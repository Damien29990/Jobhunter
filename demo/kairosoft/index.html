Style notes (Game Dev Story / 温泉物語)

• Head is ~half the sprite. Body is a stub.
• Eyes: white 2×2 + black 1×1 catch. Optional blush.
• Hair is a helmet, not strands.
• Walk is two frames + 1px bob. Never 8-frame Western cycles.
• Scale with imageSmoothingEnabled = false.
• UI: orange title bar, cream cards, chunky 3px borders.
• Talk: tiny white balloon, 2–5 words, then it pops. Random office chatter while they work.

Green sphere + ball antenna = Kairobot-class mascot (original, not a copy).
